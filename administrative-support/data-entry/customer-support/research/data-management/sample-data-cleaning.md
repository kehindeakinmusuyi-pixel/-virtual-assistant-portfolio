# Data Management — Data Cleaning and Organization

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates my ability to review, organize, clean, and standardize business data to make records easier to understand and manage.

The task involved identifying inconsistencies in a sample customer database and organizing the information into a clear and professional format.

## Client Requirements

The client needed assistance with:

- Reviewing customer records
- Identifying incomplete information
- Standardizing names and contact details
- Removing duplicate records
- Organizing information into consistent categories
- Preparing data for easier tracking and reporting

## Data Cleaning Tasks

The following tasks were completed:

1. Reviewed customer records for inconsistencies.
2. Standardized capitalization and formatting.
3. Checked contact information for missing entries.
4. Identified potential duplicate records.
5. Organized records into consistent categories.
6. Prepared the information for easy reference and future updates.
