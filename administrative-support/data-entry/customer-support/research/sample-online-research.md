# Online Research — Business Research Support

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates my ability to conduct online research, gather relevant information, verify findings, and organize research results in a clear and useful format.

The task involved researching productivity tools that could help a small remote business improve team communication, task management, and daily workflow.

## Client Research Request

The client wanted a comparison of productivity tools that could be used by a small remote team.

The research focused on:

- Main features
- Ease of use
- Team collaboration
- Task management
- Communication
- Suitability for remote work

## Research Findings

| Tool | Primary Use | Key Features | Best Suited For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trello | Task Management | Boards, cards, checklists, deadlines | Small teams |
| Slack | Team Communication | Messaging, channels, file sharing | Remote teams |
| Google Workspace | Productivity | Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Drive | Businesses and teams |
| Asana | Project Management | Tasks, projects, timelines, collaboration | Project-based teams |
| Microsoft Teams | Communication & Collaboration | Chat, meetings, file sharing | Organizations |

## Research Process

To complete the research, I:

1. Identified the client's main requirements.
2. Searched for relevant productivity tools.
3. Reviewed information from official sources.
4. Compared features based on the client's needs.
5. Organized the findings into a simple comparison table.
6. Presented the information in a format that would make decision-making easier.

## Key Recommendation

For a small remote team, the most suitable option would depend on the team's specific workflow and communication needs.

A tool focused primarily on task management may be more appropriate for teams managing multiple projects, while a communication-focused platform may be more useful for teams that need frequent real-time collaboration.
