Administrative Support — Weekly Schedule Management

Project Overview

This project demonstrates how I would support a client with weekly scheduling, task coordination, follow-ups, and administrative organization.

The objective was to maintain a clear and structured schedule, ensure priority tasks were handled on time, and keep administrative activities organized throughout the week.

Client Requirements

The client needed support with:

* Organizing weekly tasks
* Prioritizing important activities
* Scheduling meetings and appointments
* Tracking pending and completed tasks
* Following up on outstanding activities
* Keeping administrative information organized

Administrative Approach

I organized tasks according to urgency and importance, monitored pending activities, and maintained a clear record of completed work.

Regular follow-ups were included to reduce missed deadlines and ensure that important tasks remained on track.

Skills Demonstrated

The structured workflow provides the client with a clearer view of weekly priorities, completed activities, and outstanding responsibilities, helping improve organization and productivity.
